# Build checkpoints

Author: Colin Rooney · © 2026 Rooney Industries LLC · MIT licence

Choose the checkpoint for your route; the public ROS interface is independent of unpowered assembly or cradle printing. The [physical build completion list](assembly.md#build-completion-list) is the canonical list of selected-mount checks and outstanding commissioning inputs. The full physical build remains the goal, but the historical guide is not a complete commissioned recipe or a validated current controller.

Node-owner assets not supplied by this repository (model/configuration/replay images) are separate from this truck's unpublished [OVAL4 head](../model/README.md) and unavailable [sample assets](../data/README.md). OVAL4 belongs only to the historical DINO truck snapshot, separate from the manually launched field kit; neither it nor sample logs are required for synthetic ROS. The described samples exclude video/images and cannot supply a recorded-frame replay. Historical perception also needs its local DINOv2 checkout/checkpoint and verified runtime setup; see [Orin setup](orin-setup.md). Robot Eyes is **CLOSED SOURCE**; [app distribution and device access](ros.md#robot-eyes-the-phone-app) are separate prerequisites for a later live-phone path.

The [separate dated field integration](ros.md#how-it-connects-to-the-orin-stack) did have powered use; it does not validate public `truck_bridge`. Source identity alone establishes neither a reproducible public package nor fresh physical acceptance.

In the historical snapshot source, omitting `--fc` selects compute-only operation. With `--fc --dry-run`, the program connects to the flight controller and exchanges telemetry/parameter traffic while actuator override sends are disabled; this is not physical disconnection or absence of protocol traffic. <!-- src/orin/live_trail.py:673-692,1584-1590,2062-2068,2158 -->

Keep evidence distinct: written prerequisites, build/tests, actual ROS transport and process exits, replay artifacts, physical commissioning and recovery, then full fresh-follow acceptance. No checkpoint is marked passed by this page. A fresh reader must eventually complete the first drive from the tagged guide and parts list without an author answer; intermediate demos do not establish that result. The first independent fresh-follow build will take place **after public release**; no pass has been recorded. This does not relax source-owner review or powered bench/field acceptance.
