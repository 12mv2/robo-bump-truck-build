# Research record

The research record behind the build guide. A builder does not need these pages to build or drive the truck.

- [results.md](results.md): what the truck did, every number with its instrument, day and caveat.
- [method.md](method.md): how each number was measured, and each instrument's known error.
- [corrections.md](corrections.md): numbers and claims that were withdrawn or corrected, and why.
- [cannot-do-yet.md](cannot-do-yet.md): what the truck has not been shown to do.
