# Corrections

Author: Colin Rooney · © 2026 Rooney Industries LLC · MIT licence

This page lists numbers and claims from the research record that were withdrawn or corrected, and why. Each row gives what was said, what is said now, where each is recorded, and the date of the correction. Where a row replaces a figure, the figure on the other pages is the new one. The results are on [results](results.md), the instruments on [method](method.md), and the limits on [cannot-do-yet](cannot-do-yet.md).

One row, the weak direction, records two entries that disagree. It is not resolved.
